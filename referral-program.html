A warm introduction
can make a difference.

Our published referral program offers a base bonus of up to $500, with a potential EHS bonus boost of up to $250. The total potential payout is up to $750, subject to program terms.

Confirm current terms with our team before referring. EHS eligibility and acceptance must be verified for an EHS bonus.

Published program requirements

  • Referrer is a current parent, staff member, or approved community partner.
  • The child enrolls full-time and remains active for 60 consecutive calendar days.
  • No pending withdrawal or termination notice.
  • Maximum two referral payouts per person per quarter.
  • Payment follows eligibility confirmation and the retention milestone.
