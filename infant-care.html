Questions for your visit

Let’s talk about your baby’s day.

Bring your questions about rest, feeding, care routines, classroom staffing, communication, and starting care.

How young can my child start?

Please confirm the minimum enrollment age with our team before planning your start date. Our ages 0–3 focus does not mean every age has an available place.

What should I bring for infant care?

Our team will share the current supplies and paperwork checklist as part of enrollment. During a tour, ask about feeding supplies, extra clothes, and how personal items are handled.

When is infant care available?

The center operates Monday–Friday, 6 a.m.–6 p.m. Contact us to discuss your specific care schedule and current infant openings.
