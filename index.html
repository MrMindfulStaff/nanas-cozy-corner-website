A few helpful answers

Before your first hello.

Looking for another age group? Explore all of our childcare programs.

What ages should I ask about?

Our current enrollment focus is infants and toddlers, ages 0–3. Contact our team to confirm the minimum enrollment age, the right classroom, and availability for your child.

What are your hours?

Nana’s Cozy Corner operates Monday–Friday, 6 a.m.–6 p.m. Ask our team about holiday closures and the schedule you need.

How do I find out about openings and tuition?

Call or email with your child’s age, your preferred start date, and the days and hours you need. Our team will confirm availability, current rates, and any applicable support.

Can I visit before deciding?

Yes. Choose an available tour time on our website. Your visit is booked immediately, and we send a confirmation to your email.
