Early Head Start · Milwaukee

Extra support.
Right from the start.

Through our Early Head Start partnership, eligible families with children ages 0–3 may receive no-cost services that support early learning, healthy development, and family goals.

Ask about Early Head Start↗

Learning

Care and early discovery

Nurturing routines and playful experiences support children’s development in the earliest years.

Development

Support for growing children

Services may include health and developmental screenings, referrals, and support for children with disabilities.

Family

A partner in the process

Families can explore resources, goals, and next steps with program staff.

Where to begin

You don’t have to work it out alone.

Eligibility and available places are reviewed through the program. Ask about current application ages, service area, family circumstances, and required documents.

Our published EHS service area includes Milwaukee ZIP codes 53205, 53209, 53210, 53212, and 53216. Contact the team to confirm current coverage.

General Early Head Start services may also support expectant families; ask the program about the option that fits your needs.

A first question is enough.

This website does not determine eligibility or complete an EHS application. You do not need to share benefit, income, medical, or housing documents here.

For program information, visit Wisconsin Early Childhood Association, or contact our team for help getting started.
