Information you choose to share

Our inquiry helpers ask for your name, a phone number or email, and basic care or visit preferences. Do not include children’s full names, medical records, benefit documents, Social Security numbers, or payment details.

Email preparation and delivery

If the button says “Review email,” the website prepares a message on your device. Nothing is sent until you send it through your email service. You can also call or email our team directly.

If direct online submission is available, the form will say “Send request.” Those requests are processed by our website host, Vercel, and delivered through Resend to the center’s inquiry inbox. Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect direct submissions from automated abuse. A request is used to respond to your inquiry; it does not automatically sign you up for marketing.

Technical records

Our hosting provider processes technical request information to deliver and protect the website. We do not put the contact details or child information entered in these forms into marketing analytics. We do not save form contents in browser local storage.

Questions

To ask about information you have shared or request a correction or deletion, contact Info@nanascozycorner.com or 414-442-6262. We will discuss your request and any applicable recordkeeping requirements with you.
